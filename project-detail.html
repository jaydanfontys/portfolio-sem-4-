Project Brief

We had to make a music video for the artist Serieus?! for his new song Zandloper. I had the role of camera operator and editor for this project.

We had to use virtual production for this project because the artist wanted a 3D environment for the music video. We used Unreal Engine, and I made the opening scene for the project inside Unreal Engine. I also helped film the music video with a professional camera, and I edited and color graded the trailer.
